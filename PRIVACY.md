# Segno privacy policy

The full privacy policy, for both the app and the website, is at
**https://segnomusic.pages.dev/privacy**. In short:

Segno doesn't collect, store or send any information about you. There are no accounts,
no analytics and no ads, and Segno has no server of its own.

Everything Segno keeps stays on your computer: your settings, playlists, loved songs, queue,
download list, saved cover art, saved lyrics, songs you played online (so they load faster next time), and
a list of the last 300 songs you played (kept for a future feature that suggests music).

Segno connects to the internet only to:

- search, play and download from YouTube and other sites you search or paste links from
- read the public Spotify page when you paste a Spotify link
- look up synced lyrics on lrclib.net when you open the lyrics panel (it gets the song title, artist, album and length; turn it off in Settings and nothing is sent)
- download yt-dlp (and Deno, if needed) to do that
- check GitHub for new versions (not in the Microsoft Store version, which gets updates from the Store)

Those sites see the requests Segno makes for you, the same as when you visit them in a browser.
